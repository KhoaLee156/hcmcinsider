Bún bò Phú Bình: a gentle bowl of Hue beef noodles

A morning-only Hue-style beef noodle shop in Phú Bình with a clean, sweet, spicy broth that is easy on first-timers. 50,000 dong a bowl.

By HCMC Insider · 31 October 2026

This shop is also in the Phú Bình parish area of old District 11, a few minutes from Hỷ Ký. A bowl costs 50,000 dong and comes with rare beef (tái), brisket (nạm), chả and tendon (gân). You can also add pork knuckle (giò) or stewed beef bones (xương bò hầm).

It is Hue-style bún bò. The broth is clean and gently sweet, with a warm, spicy kick. It doesn't pile on the seasoning the way some places do; some shops add more fermented shrimp paste (mắm ruốc) or season heavily with fish sauce.

That is why I think it suits visitors: if this is your first bún bò, the broth here is a gentle place to start.

The shop sells in the morning only.

Verdict: very worth trying.

Practical info: Bún bò Phú Bình, in alley 423 Lạc Long Quân, Hòa Bình ward (the Phú Bình area of old District 11). The sign out on the street gives the address as 20W Lạc Long Quân. 50,000 dong a bowl. Mornings only.
