HCMC Insider: Ho Chi Minh City food, sights and local tips

HCMC Insider is an independent English guide to Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) by locals. It lists 69 food spots, markets and sights, mostly in Cholon (Saigon Chinatown), each with its Vietnamese name, address, directions and links to local creators' videos, plus practical guides to the metro, free city buses and the airport bus.
