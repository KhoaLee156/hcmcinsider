Ben Thanh Market

Chợ Bến Thành

Is it worth visiting? Worth a look for the building and the food stalls, but it is the city's most touristy market: prices for souvenirs start high. The market runs from about 6 am to 6 pm, then a night market sets up on the side streets until about 10 pm. Metro Line 1 starts right next to it.

At a glance

Hours
About 6:00 am – 6:00 pm; night market on the side streets until about 10:00 pm
Entry
Free
Time needed
45–90 minutes

Why go

  • The current building with its famous clock tower was finished in 1914 and is one of Saigon's symbols.
  • Inside you will find clothes, coffee, dried fruit and a food section where you can try many Vietnamese dishes under one roof.
  • After 6 pm, stalls on Phan Bội Châu and Phan Chu Trinh streets add an evening market with more food.

How to get there

  • Metro Line 1: Ben Thanh station is right next to the market.
  • Airport bus 152 runs via Ben Thanh Market; city buses are free until 31 Dec 2026.
  • Ride-hailing: ask for “Chợ Bến Thành”.

Local tips

  • Ask the price before you order or buy, and bargain politely for souvenirs.
  • Crowds attract pickpockets: carry your bag in front.
  • For more local markets, head to Cholon: Binh Tay, Phung Hung and Hoa Binh.
