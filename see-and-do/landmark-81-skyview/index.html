Landmark 81 SkyView

Đài quan sát Landmark 81 SkyView

Is it worth visiting? Yes, for the highest view in the city: the observation deck is on floors 79–81 of Vietnam's tallest building. Go late afternoon to see sunset and the lights come on, and come by metro to Tan Cang station. Tickets are pricier at weekends, so a weekday visit saves money.

Why go

  • 360° views over the Saigon River, District 1's skyline and the new Thu Thiem area.
  • A glass floor, telescopes and the 'Skytouch' open-air challenge on the top floor, with safety gear.
  • Bars and restaurants on floor 79 if you want to stay for a drink after dark.

How to get there

  • Metro Line 1: Tan Cang station, then a short walk to Vinhomes Central Park.
  • Ride-hailing: ask for “Landmark 81”, about 20–25 minutes from Ben Thanh depending on traffic.
  • Ticket counter: basement B1 of Vincom Landmark 81.

Local tips

  • Check the weather: on hazy or rainy days the view drops a lot.
  • Drinking water and dangerous items are not allowed in; finish your drink before security.
  • Weekday tickets are cheaper and the deck is quieter.
