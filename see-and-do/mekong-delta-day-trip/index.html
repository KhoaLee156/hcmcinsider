Mekong Delta day trip

Du lịch miền Tây: Mỹ Tho – Bến Tre

Is it worth visiting? Yes, if you want a taste of river life without staying overnight. A day trip to My Tho and Ben Tre means about two hours on the road each way, then boats between coconut-palm islands, fruit, honey tea and folk music. It is touristy but pleasant; for real floating markets you need to stay a night near Can Tho.

Current status

Most day tours leave District 1 around 7:00 am and are back in the late afternoon. A typical route: boat from My Tho pier, coconut candy workshop, Thoi Son island with honey tea and a horse cart, sampan ride with fruit and music, lunch on Con Phung, and Vinh Trang Pagoda on the way back (iVIVU tour sheet).

At a glance

Time needed
A full day, roughly 7:00 am – 5:00 pm
Travel
About 2 hours each way by road
Best for
A first look at the delta without staying overnight
Floating markets
Not on most day trips; Cai Rang (Can Tho) needs an overnight stay

Why go

  • A boat ride on the wide Tien River past the 'four sacred animal' islands and the Rach Mieu bridge.
  • Paddling a small sampan through narrow coconut-palm canals, the photo everyone comes for.
  • Coconut candy workshops, honey tea, tropical fruit and đờn ca tài tử, the traditional music of the south.

How to get there

  • Day tour with hotel or District 1 pickup: by far the easiest, with boats, lunch and guide included.
  • Combined Cu Chi Tunnels + Mekong day tours exist but are rushed; pick one if you can.
  • Going alone: intercity buses leave from the Mien Tay bus station; you then arrange boats at My Tho pier yourself.

Local tips

  • Bring sunscreen, a hat and insect repellent; boats have little shade.
  • Carry small notes for tips to the sampan rowers and musicians.
  • Small-group tours visit the same islands but feel much less crowded.
