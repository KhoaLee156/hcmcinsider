Saigon River dinner cruise

Du thuyền ăn tối trên sông Sài Gòn

Is it worth visiting? Worth it if you want an easy, relaxed evening: two hours on the river with dinner and the lit-up skyline. The food is buffet or set menus rather than the city's best, so treat it as a view with dinner. Book ahead for weekends and holidays.

Current status

Dinner cruises usually board around 6:30–7:00 pm and return around 9:00–9:30 pm. Buffet cruises start at about 500,000 dong per person, with higher decks and fine-dining boats costing more; drinks and VAT are often extra (Vietnambooking, MIA.vn).

Why go

  • The best way to see the skyline at night, including Landmark 81 and the Thu Thiem bridges.
  • Buffet or set-menu dinner on board, often with live music.
  • A calm break from motorbike traffic, good for families and couples.

How to get there

  • Piers are in District 1 (around Bach Dang) and District 4 (near Nha Rong wharf); your ticket names the exact pier.
  • Ride-hailing is easiest; arrive 30 minutes before departure.
  • On a budget, the river bus from Bach Dang pier gives similar views for a fraction of the price (no dinner).

Local tips

  • Check exactly what is included: drinks, VAT and transfers vary a lot between boats.
  • Ask for an upper or outdoor deck for the best views.
  • Bring a light layer: it can be windy on deck.
