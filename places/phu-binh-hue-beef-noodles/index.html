Phu Binh Hue Beef Noodles

Bún bò Phú Bình

Morning-only Hue-style beef noodle shop in the Phú Bình area, with a lighter, clean-tasting broth that first-timers tend to like. 50,000 dong a bowl.

Hours
Mornings only
Price
50,000 dong a bowl
What to order
A normal bowl comes with rare beef, brisket, chả (pork sausage) and tendon; extras include pork knuckle (giò) and stewed beef bones
Good to know
Lighter broth than many bún bò shops, with less shrimp paste and fish sauce: a good first bowl for visitors

What is bún bò Huế?

Bún bò Huế comes from the old imperial city of Hue: round rice noodles in a beef broth scented with lemongrass and chilli, with beef shank and pork. It is spicier and punchier than pho.
