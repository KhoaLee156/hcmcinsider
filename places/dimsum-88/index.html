What is dim sum (điểm tâm)?

Dim sum are small Cantonese dishes served in steamers or on plates: há cảo (har gow, shrimp dumplings), xíu mại (siu mai), buns and rice rolls. In Saigon it is mostly a morning and weekend meal, best shared.
