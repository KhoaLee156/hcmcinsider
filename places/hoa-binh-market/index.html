Hoa Binh Market

Chợ Hòa Bình

Historic Chinese market in District 5 for food and shopping, with fried noodle stalls at night and Ma Nghia's coffee.

DirectionsWatch video

Local tips

What to order
Inside the market hall: vegetarian stir-fried vermicelli (about 30,000 dong) and sâm bổ lượng iced sweet soup (about 20,000 dong)
Good to know
One of Saigon's old markets with distinctive architecture, and a good place to buy dried goods for Chinese cooking. In the evening, stalls nearby sell stir-fried noodles.

From the video descriptions by CuongCanCook Official, VINH LAM TV. Hours and prices change, so check before you go.
