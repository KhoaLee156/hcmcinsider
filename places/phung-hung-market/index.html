Phung Hung Market

Chợ Phùng Hưng

Morning market in the heart of Cholon. Try sticky rice, steamed buns, Hong Kong-style rice rolls, chive cakes and old-school sock-filtered coffee. Busiest 6–9am.

DirectionsWatch video

Local tips

Hours
Busiest 6am – 9am
What to order
Sticky rice from the Lan Anh stall (20,000–25,000 dong; the vendor has sold here for 49 years), Hong Kong-style rice rolls with pork, shrimp, scallop or crab (28,000–47,000 dong), Ba Lù coffee, Tăng bánh mì and the stuffed bitter melon and chilli stall
Good to know
Locals also call it chợ Thủ Đô or the "rich people's market".

From the video descriptions by CuongCanCook Official, Tan Vi Vu, Wego, Én điệu ham vui. Hours and prices change, so check before you go.
