Chicken noodles · Cholon (District 11)

Hy Ky Chicken Noodles

Mì gà xá kén Hỷ Ký

Morning-only chicken noodle shop in a Phú Bình alley, known for boiled free-range chicken with a chilli dipping sauce of chopped xá kén. Our own pick of the five or six chicken noodle shops in the area.

Hours
Mornings only; usually sold out by about 11 am
Price
50,000 dong for a normal bowl; quarter and half chickens available
What to order
Mì gà xá kén. Taste the chicken plain first, then dip it in xá kén with chilli sauce and green chilli
Good to know
Narrow alley with few tables: come early or expect to wait

What is mì gà?

Mì gà is a Chinese-Vietnamese chicken noodle soup: springy yellow egg noodles in a clear chicken broth with boiled chicken. In the Phú Bình area of old District 11 it is served with xá kén, a chopped root you mix into a chilli dipping sauce for the chicken.
