What is chay?

Chay means vegetarian, usually Buddhist-style: no meat, no fish sauce, and often no garlic or onion. Chay shops make noodle soups, rice plates and mock meats from tofu, mushrooms and wheat gluten. They are busiest on the 1st and 15th days of the lunar month.
