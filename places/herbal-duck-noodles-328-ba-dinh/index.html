What is mì vịt tiềm?

Mì vịt tiềm is a Chinese-Vietnamese noodle soup: a duck leg slow-braised in a dark broth with Chinese medicinal herbs, served with springy egg noodles and greens. It is a Cholon classic, rich but not spicy.
