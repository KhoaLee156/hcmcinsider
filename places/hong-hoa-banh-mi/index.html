What is bánh mì?

Bánh mì is Saigon's famous sandwich: a light, crackly baguette filled with pâté, cold cuts or grilled pork, pickled carrot and daikon, cucumber, coriander and chilli. Many shops also serve bánh mì chảo or ốp la, eggs and meats in a hot pan with bread on the side for breakfast.
