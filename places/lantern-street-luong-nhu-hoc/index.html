Lantern Street (Luong Nhu Hoc)

Phố lồng đèn Lương Nhữ Học

Saigon's lantern street. It glows with thousands of lanterns in the weeks before the Mid-Autumn Festival.

Local tips

Hours
Best in the weeks before the Mid-Autumn Festival, in the evening
Good to know
The lantern area spreads over Lương Nhữ Học, Hải Thượng Lãn Ông and Phú Định streets, where handmade lantern-making has been passed down through generations.

From the video descriptions by HTV Thành phố không ngủ, Cecilia Ninh. Hours and prices change, so check before you go.
