Privacy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What we collect

We collect no personal data ourselves: this site has no accounts and no sign-up. The “Suggest a spot” form only builds a message on your screen; nothing is sent until you choose to send it yourself.

What stays in your browser

Places you tap “Save” on are stored in your own browser (local storage) so you can find them again. They never leave your device. Clear your browser data to remove them.

To make saved places work offline, your browser keeps copies of this site's pages (a service worker cache) on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

“Near me, open now” asks your browser for your location only when you tap it. Distances are worked out on your phone; your location is never sent to us or stored. The order card only builds Vietnamese text on your screen.

The QR code maker builds every code in your browser; what you type is never sent to us. Codes you save or share are kept in a history list in your own browser (local storage) until you delete them. When you scan with the camera, the picture is read on your device and never recorded or sent. If your browser cannot read QR codes by itself, it downloads an open-source QR reader (zxing-wasm) from the jsDelivr network the first time you scan; see the jsDelivr privacy policy.

Third-party services

  • Visitor statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across sites. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap; your browser requests them directly. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Fonts (Be Vietnam Pro) are served from our own site, so loading a page makes no request to Google Fonts or any other font service.
  • Videos open on YouTube, Facebook or other platforms under their own terms. Video titles, channel names and view counts come from the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Ask a local chat: when you ask a question in the chat, your question and the conversation so far are sent to Anthropic, whose Claude AI writes the answer from this site's guides. We do not store the conversation; it stays in your browser tab until you close it. Please don't include personal details. See the Anthropic privacy policy.
  • Affiliate links to booking and travel services are managed by Travelpayouts. When you click one, Travelpayouts and the partner (for example Agoda) may set cookies to credit the referral. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; it never changes which places we list. See the Travelpayouts privacy policy and cookie policy.

Creators and businesses

If you made a video listed here, or own a place we list, and want it changed or removed, email hello@hcmcinsider.com and we will act within 7 days.
