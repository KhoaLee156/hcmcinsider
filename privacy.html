Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking cookies. The “Suggest a spot” form only builds a message on your screen; nothing is sent until you choose to send it yourself.

What stays in your browser

Places you tap “Save” on are stored in your own browser (local storage) so you can find them again. They never leave your device. Clear your browser data to remove them.

Third-party services

  • Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap; your browser requests them directly. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Videos open on YouTube, Facebook or other platforms under their own terms. Video titles, channel names and view counts come from the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Creators and businesses

If you made a video listed here, or own a place we list, and want it changed or removed, contact us and we will act within 7 days.
