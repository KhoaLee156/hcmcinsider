Where to stay in Ho Chi Minh City: the best areas for visitors

Saigon is big and traffic is slow, so where you sleep decides how much of your trip is spent in a car. Here are the four areas we would pick, who each one suits, and the catch with each.

Short answer: first visit of 2–4 days, stay in District 1 near Ben Thanh or the Opera House, but a few blocks away from Bui Vien. Want a quieter base that is still central, pick District 3. Here mainly to eat, stay in Cholon. Staying a week or more, or with kids, look at Thao Dien.

District 1 (Ben Thanh, Nguyen Hue)

Best for: First visit, short stays, walking to the sights

  • Walk to Ben Thanh Market, Nguyen Hue, the Opera House, Notre-Dame and the Post Office.
  • Metro Line 1 starts here (Ben Thanh and Opera House stations), and airport bus 152 stops at Ben Thanh.
  • Widest choice of hotels, from hostels to five-star riverside towers.

Keep in mind: Busiest and priciest area. Streets around Bui Vien (the backpacker quarter) are loud until late: pick a hotel a few blocks away if you want to sleep.

District 3

Best for: A quieter base next to the centre

  • Leafy streets, old villas and local cafés, 10–20 minutes on foot from District 1.
  • Close to the War Remnants Museum and the Independence Palace.
  • Often better value than District 1 for similar hotels.

Keep in mind: Fewer big hotels; more boutique and serviced-apartment style. Nightlife is limited compared with District 1.

Cholon (Districts 5, 6 and 11)

Best for: Food lovers and repeat visitors

  • Saigon's Chinatown, where most of the food spots on this site are.
  • Temples, Binh Tay Market and old trade streets on your doorstep.
  • Hotels are noticeably cheaper than in District 1.

Keep in mind: About 20–30 minutes by car from District 1; the metro does not reach Cholon yet (bus 1 does). Little English and few international-style hotels.

Thao Dien (Thu Duc)

Best for: Longer stays, families, a calmer pace

  • The expat quarter: cafés, brunch spots, boutiques and riverside restaurants.
  • Metro Line 1 (Thao Dien station) reaches Ben Thanh in about 15 minutes.
  • More space, green streets and serviced apartments for longer stays.

Keep in mind: Far from Cholon and the old-town sights. Fewer budget options.

Tips for booking

  • Check the map, not the name: many hotels call themselves "District 1" while being a long walk from the sights.
  • Ask for a room away from the street side if you are a light sleeper; motorbike traffic starts early.
  • Near a metro station is a real plus for getting east to Thao Dien and Landmark 81.
  • Arriving late? Book an airport transfer to the hotel door.
