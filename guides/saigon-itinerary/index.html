Planning · Itinerary

Saigon itinerary: plan 1, 2 or 3 days in Ho Chi Minh City

Pick how many days you have and what you enjoy. The plan groups stops by area so you are not stuck in traffic, puts the morning-only food stalls in the morning, and keeps the hottest hours for lunch and a rest.

Day 1 · District 1 and District 3

Old Saigon on foot

The palace, the museum and the French-era landmarks are all within walking distance. Start early: it is cooler and the queues are short.

  1. 7:30 am

    Breakfast

    Hong Hoa Banh Mi Bánh mì Hồng HoaBanh mi · District 1Or: Hoa Ma Banh Mi, My Kim Noodles

    Banh mi or a bowl of noodles, the way Saigon starts the day.

  2. 8:30 am
    Independence Palace Dinh Độc LậpMain building 8:00 am – 4:30 pm; exhibition 8:30 am – 4:30 pm; tickets until 3:30 pm

    Go when it opens, before the tour groups. Allow 1–1.5 hours.

  3. 10:15 am
    War Remnants Museum Bảo tàng Chứng tích Chiến tranh7:30 am – 5:30 pm daily; ticket counter closes 5:00 pm

    About a 10-minute walk. Allow 1.5–2 hours; it is a hard but important visit.

  4. 1:00 pm

    Lunch

    Kieu Ky Har Gow Há cảo Kiều KýDim sum · District 1 · 1pm – 10pmOr: Hoa Ma Banh Mi

    Then rest in the hotel or a café through the hottest hours.

  5. 3:00 pm
    Saigon Notre-Dame Cathedral Nhà thờ Chính tòa Đức Bà Sài Gòn + Saigon Central Post Office Bưu điện Trung tâm Sài GònAbout 7:00 am – 7:00 pm daily, including most holidays (checked January 2026)

    Side by side: the cathedral from outside, then the Post Office hall.

  6. 4:00 pm
    Ben Thanh Market Chợ Bến ThànhAbout 6:00 am – 6:00 pm; night market on the side streets until about 10:00 pm

    About 15 minutes on foot. Stalls start closing around 6 pm.

  7. 7:30 pm
    Nguyen Hue Walking Street and the Opera House Phố đi bộ Nguyễn Huệ · Nhà hát Thành phốOpen 24 hours

    The evening walk with locals, from the Opera House end down to the river.

Day 2 · Districts 5, 6 and 11

Cholon: Chinatown food, temples and markets

Saigon's Chinatown is 20–30 minutes by car from District 1 (or city bus 1). Come hungry and early: many of the best stalls are mornings only.

  1. 6:30 am

    Breakfast

    Phu Binh Hue Beef Noodles Bún bò Phú BìnhSpicy Hue beef noodles · Cholon (District 11) · Mornings onlyOr: Hy Ky Chicken Noodles, Nguu Ky Noodles

    Morning-only noodle shops: go before 9 am, some sell out.

  2. 8:00 am
    Binh Tay Market Chợ Bình Tây

    Busiest and most alive in the early morning.

  3. 9:30 am

    Wet market

    Phung Hung Market Chợ Phùng HưngLocal market · Cholon (District 5) · Busiest 6am – 9amOr: Hoa Binh Market

    A local fresh market: busiest from 6 to 9 am, so this is the tail end.

  4. 10:30 am
    Thien Hau Temple (Tue Thanh Assembly Hall) Chùa Bà Thiên Hậu · Hội quán Tuệ ThànhAbout 6:30 am – 4:30 pm

    Look up at the incense coils. About 30 minutes.

  5. 11:00 am

    Old streets

    Lantern Street (Luong Nhu Hoc) Phố lồng đèn Lương Nhữ HọcLantern street · Cholon (District 5) · Best in the weeks before the Mid-Autumn Festival, in the eveningOr: Herbal Medicine Street

    A short walk from the temple.

  6. 11:45 am

    Lunch

    Dong Nguyen Chicken Rice Cơm gà Đông NguyênChicken rice · CholonOr: Shan Dimsum, Teochew Rice Shop

    Chicken rice, dim sum or a Teochew rice shop.

  7. 1:30 pm

    Café break

    No. 1 Tan Da Antique Café Cà phê số 1 Tản ĐàCafé · Cholon (District 5)Or: Chuyen Ca Phe, CIELO Cholon

    Sit out the midday heat with a Vietnamese iced coffee.

  8. 3:30 pm

    Sweet soup

    Ha Ky Sweet Soups Chè Hà KýSweet soup desserts · Cholon (District 5) · 10am – 10pmOr: Yukii Desserts, Lam Thanh Sweet Soups

    Chè: Chinese-style sweet soups, hot or iced.

  9. 5:30 pm

    Dinner

    Phat Ky Phát KýChinese-Vietnamese kitchen · Cholon · 4pm – 10:30pmOr: 8,000-dong Noodle Cart, Phuong's Hue Beef Noodles

    Evening stalls open from about 4–5 pm.

Questions visitors ask

How many days do you need in Ho Chi Minh City?

Two full days covers the main landmarks in District 1 and a day of food and temples in Cholon. A third day adds the Cu Chi Tunnels or a Mekong Delta day trip.

Can you walk between the sights in District 1?

Yes. The Independence Palace, War Remnants Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Central Post Office, Ben Thanh Market and Nguyen Hue Walking Street are all within about 20 minutes on foot of each other. Use Grab for Cholon and Landmark 81.

What is the best time of day for sightseeing in Saigon?

Early morning and late afternoon. It is hot from late morning to mid-afternoon, so plan lunch, a café or a rest in that window, as this planner does.
