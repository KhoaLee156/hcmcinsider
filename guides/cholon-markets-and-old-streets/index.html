Markets and old streets of Cholon

Short answer: if you only have time for a few, start with the spots local creators' videos have been watched most: Thiec Market (local market), Phung Hung Market (local market) and Lantern Street (Luong Nhu Hoc) (lantern street). Every place below shows its Vietnamese name, address and directions.

Cholon's markets and trade streets are where the neighbourhood's daily life happens. Binh Tay Market is the grand old wholesale market; smaller wet markets like Hoa Binh and Phung Hung are where locals buy breakfast, snacks and fresh food.

Some streets still specialise in one trade: lanterns on Luong Nhu Hoc (especially before the Mid-Autumn Festival) and traditional Chinese medicine on Hai Thuong Lan Ong, where the air smells of dried herbs.

Before you go

  • Markets are busiest and most interesting in the early morning.
  • Keep your phone and bag on the side away from passing motorbikes.
  • Ask before photographing vendors, and buy a snack if you linger at a stall.
