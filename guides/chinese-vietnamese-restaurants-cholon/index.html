Chinese restaurants and chicken rice in Saigon Chinatown

Short answer: if you only have time for a few, start with the spots local creators' videos have been watched most: Dong Nguyen Chicken Rice (chicken rice), Huy Ky (chinese-vietnamese kitchen) and Phat Ky (chinese-vietnamese kitchen). Every place below shows its Vietnamese name, address and directions.

Beyond street stalls, Cholon is full of family restaurants cooking Teochew and Cantonese dishes for big tables: stir-fries, soups, roast meats and rice plates. Many have been run by the same family for decades.

Chicken rice (cơm gà) is a local favourite: poached or crispy-fried chicken with rice cooked in chicken fat and ginger sauce. Go with a group to order several dishes and share.

Before you go

  • Big menus are often only in Vietnamese and Chinese; a translation app's camera mode helps.
  • Lunch and early dinner are the busiest times.
  • Ask the price of seafood dishes before ordering; they are often priced by weight.
