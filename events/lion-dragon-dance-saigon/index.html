Lion and dragon dance in Saigon

Múa lân – sư – rồng

Can visitors see it? Yes, if your trip falls on the right dates. Lion (lân), lion (sư, the northern Chinese style) and dragon (rồng) troupes perform for luck at the Lunar New Year, the Lantern Festival and the Mid-Autumn Festival, and at shop openings all year. Cholon, Saigon's Chinatown, has the most troupes and the best chance of seeing one.

Current status

The best fixed dates are the Lunar New Year (from Saturday 6 February 2027), the Lantern Festival parade in Cholon (Saturday 20 February 2027) and the Mid-Autumn Festival. Outside those dates you may still meet troupes at shop openings, especially in Cholon.

At a glance

When
Lunar New Year, Lantern Festival (20 Feb 2027), Mid-Autumn Festival, shop openings
Where
Cholon streets and assembly halls; malls and hotels at Tet
Cost
Free to watch
Sound
Loud drums: bring ear plugs for small children

What you'll see

  • Drums, cymbals and gongs set the rhythm while two dancers inside each lion leap, bow and 'sleep'; acrobatic troupes balance on tall poles.
  • The lion 'plucks the greens' (lettuce and a red envelope hung over a door) to bring luck to the shop or home.
  • Dragons are carried on poles by a team of dancers, chasing a pearl through the street.
  • Some Cholon troupes also bring out the Ông Địa (Earth God) character, who fans and teases the lion.

How to get there

  • Cholon is 20–30 minutes from District 1 by car; bus 1 also goes there.
  • At Tet, malls and big hotels in District 1 often book troupes in the first days of the year.
  • For the biggest show, follow the Lantern Festival parade in Cholon.

Local tips

  • Follow the drums: you will usually hear a troupe before you see it.
  • Don't step between the lion and the greens it is trying to reach.
  • Small red envelopes (lì xì) for the troupe are welcome but not expected from visitors.
