Tết (Lunar New Year) in Saigon 2027

Tết Nguyên Đán Đinh Mùi 2027

Is it a good time to visit? Yes, with planning. The first day of the Year of the Goat (Đinh Mùi) is Saturday 6 February 2027. The week before is festive and busy; then for the first days of the new year the city empties out, many family shops close and the streets are calm and decorated. Book transport and rooms early, and expect some places on this site to be closed.

Current status

Tet 2027: New Year's Eve is Friday 5 February, the first day is Saturday 6 February. The official public-holiday schedule for 2027 had not been announced as of October 2026. Many small family shops close for several days around the first day; malls, big restaurants, hotels and tourist sights mostly stay open, sometimes with shorter hours or a holiday surcharge.

What makes it special

  • The days before Tet: flower markets, kumquat and apricot-blossom trees on every motorbike, and streets hung with red lanterns.
  • New Year's Eve (the night of 5 February 2027): the city usually holds fireworks at several sites at midnight.
  • The first days of the year: quiet streets, people in new clothes visiting pagodas and family, and lion dances for luck.
  • Two weeks later, the Lantern Festival (20 February 2027) brings Cholon's biggest parade.

How to get there

  • Book flights, trains and buses out of the city weeks ahead; they sell out before Tet.
  • Ride-hailing still works over Tet, but expect higher prices and longer waits.
  • Arriving around Tet? Pre-book your airport transfer; see the airport to city guide.

Local tips

  • Check opening days before crossing town to a restaurant; many family shops close over the first days.
  • Say “Chúc mừng năm mới” (Happy New Year) to the people you meet.
  • Give and receive red envelopes (lì xì) with both hands; new, crisp notes are the custom.
  • Avoid sweeping, arguing or lending money on the first day; locals take first-day luck seriously.
